TITLE 2. COMPENSATORY RELIEF [3281 - 3361] CHAPTER 1. Damages in General [3281 - 3296] ARTICLE 1. General Principles [3281 - 3283] 3281. Every person who suffers detriment from the unlawful act or omission of another, may recover from the person in fault a compensation therefor in money, which is called damages.(Enacted 1872.)
