Info Page Health Care Security OrdinanceEmployers with 20 or more workers must spend a minimum amount on health care for employees who work 8 or more hours per week.Office of Labor Standards EnforcementUpdate: 2027 Health Care Expenditure RatesStarting January 1, 2027, the required Health Care Expenditure Rates for large businesses (100+ employees) will be $4.49 per hour. The rate for medium-sized businesses (20-99 employees) will be $2.99.Managerial, supervisory, and confidential employees who earn more than $131,763 per year (or $63.35 per hour) are exempt.OverviewUnder the Health Care Security Ordinance (HCSO), all covered employers must meet the following obligations:Satisfy the Employer Spending Requirement by making required health care expenditures on a quarterly basis on behalf of all covered employees.Check the employer expenditure rate table for current rates.Covered employees are those who have been employed for more than 90 days and who regularly work at least 8 hours per week in San Francisco.Maintain records to show that you are in compliance with HCSO.Post the 2026 HCSO Poster (effective January 1, 2026) in all workplaces with covered employees.Submit a HCSO Annual Reporting Form to the OLSE by May 2, 2025.Employer expenditure ratesFind current and recent expenditure rates in the table provided. We also have an accessible version of the expenditure rate table.Exemption Threshold: 2026 Exemption Threshold: managerial, supervisory, and confidential employees who earn more than $128,861 per year (or $61.95 per hour) are exempt. Starting January 1, 2027, managerial, supervisory, and confidential employees who earn more than $ 131,763 per year (or $63.35 per hour) are exempt.Compliance optionsEmployers can choose how to spend the money on health care for their employees. Some of the common expenses include:Payments for health, dental, and/or vision insurancePayments to the SF City Option To learn more about contributing to the SF City Option, attend a training, visit the SF City Option website, or contact SF City Option at employerservices@sfcityoption.org or (415) 615-4492.​Contributions to programs that reimburse employees for out-of-pocket health care costsLegal authorityAdministrative Code and RulesSan Francisco L.E.C.
