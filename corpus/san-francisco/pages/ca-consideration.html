DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91] PART 2. CONTRACTS [1549 - 1701] TITLE 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT [1549 - 1615] CHAPTER 4. Object of a Contract [1595 - 1599] 1596. The object of a contract must be lawful when the contract is made, and possible and ascertainable by the time the contract is to be performed.(Enacted 1872.)
