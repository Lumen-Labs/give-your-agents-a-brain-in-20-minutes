DIVISION 3. OBLIGATIONS [1427 - 3273.91] PART 2. CONTRACTS [1549 - 1701] TITLE 1. NATURE OF A CONTRACT [1549 - 1615] CHAPTER 1. Definition [1549 - 1550.5] 1550. It is essential to the existence of a contract that there should be:1. Parties capable of contracting;2. Their consent;3. A lawful object; and,4. A sufficient cause or consideration.(Enacted 1872.)
