Small Business Exemption UpdateProposition M set the Small Business Exemption for the Commercial Rents Tax at $2,325,000 for 2025. Businesses with gross receipts of $2,325,000 or less are exempt from this tax. This amount is lower than the $5,000,000 small business exemption that applies to most other Annual Business Taxes, such as the Gross Receipts Tax, Homelessness Gross Receipts Tax, and Overpaid Executive Tax. Example: If a business leases out commercial space in the City and has $4 million in San Francisco gross receipts, it will be required to file the Commercial Rents Tax, even though it may be exempt from filing the Gross Receipts Tax and other taxes with a $5 million small business exemption threshold.
